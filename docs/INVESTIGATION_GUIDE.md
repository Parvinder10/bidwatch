# BIDWATCH: Human Investigator & Procurement Auditor Field Guide

## 1. Operating Instructions for Vigilance & Audit Teams
This guide provides practical investigative SOPs for procurement auditors, vigilance officers, internal audit wings, and investigative journalists evaluating leads generated by **BIDWATCH**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Ethical & Legal Audit Principle:**
> A statistical indicator flagged by BIDWATCH is **not proof of wrongdoing or corruption**. Many anomalies have legitimate operational explanations (e.g., specialized proprietary defense tech, natural disaster emergencies requiring expedited tenders, or severe geological conditions necessitating variation orders). Use this platform as an intelligence radar to direct limited audit resources toward high-yield records.

---

## 2. Investigative Playbooks by Indicator

### Playbook 1: Investigating High Concentration & Repeat Winners (`FLAG_HHI_CONCENTRATION`, `FLAG_REPEAT_WINNER`)
- **Step 1: Obtain the Standard Bidding Document (SBD)**:
  Examine Section III (Evaluation & Qualification Criteria). Check if qualifying turnover, equipment ownership, or previous completion criteria mirror the exact past projects of the winning contractor.
- **Step 2: Inspect Technical Disqualification Logs**:
  Review the Technical Evaluation Committee (TEC) minutes. Did competing bidders get disqualified on non-material, curable procedural grounds (e.g., missing page numbers, minor stamp format)?
- **Step 3: Conflict of Interest Declarations**:
  Verify whether members of the Tender Committee filed formal non-conflict declarations under Rule 175 of GFR 2017.
- **Step 4: Market Outreach**:
  Verify if pre-bid notices were published in widely circulated newspapers or solely in obscure gazettes.

---

### Playbook 2: Investigating Post-Award Value Escalation (`FLAG_VALUE_ESCALATION`)
- **Step 1: Check Delegation of Financial Powers**:
  Determine if the original tender was deliberately sized just below a higher administrative approval threshold (e.g., sized at ₹4.95 Cr when ₹5.0 Cr requires Ministry sanction), and subsequently escalated via amendments.
- **Step 2: Review Variation Approval Certificates**:
  Audit the site engineer's technical justification. Were the "extra items" foreseeable at the Detailed Project Report (DPR) stage?
- **Step 3: Item Rate Disparity Analysis**:
  Compare the unit prices of the escalated items against the original non-escalated items. (Check for "unbalanced bidding" where artificially inflated items were expanded post-award while low-priced items were reduced).

---

### Playbook 3: Investigating Compressed Bidding Windows (`FLAG_SHORT_BID_WINDOW`)
- **Step 1: Examine Urgency Certificate**:
  Check if a formal "Certificate of Urgency" was signed by the Head of Department prior to publication.
- **Step 2: Publication Timing**:
  Check if the tender was published on a Friday evening or right before a national holiday weekend, effectively reducing genuine preparation time to 2-3 working days.
- **Step 3: Extension Requests**:
  Check portal logs for vendor requests for deadline extensions and review the administrative rationale for rejecting extensions.

---

### Playbook 4: Investigating Single-Bidder Awards (`FLAG_SINGLE_BIDDER`)
- **Step 1: Reason for Non-Retendering**:
  Verify why the procuring entity did not follow CVC guidelines to cancel and retender with relaxed terms when only a single bid was received.
- **Step 2: Price Reasonableness Determination**:
  Inspect the rate analysis on file. Did the purchase committee benchmark the single bid against current market rates or did they accept an inflated quote?
